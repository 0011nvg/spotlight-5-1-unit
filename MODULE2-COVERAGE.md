## Speaking Arcade

1. Passport interview — You arrive at a new language club. Introduce yourself and learn about another traveller.
2. The hero interview — A magazine wants an interview with your invented hero. Keep a secret identity.
3. The lost school bag — A bag is at the school office. Find out whether it is yours.
4. Borrow it politely — You need something for a lesson. Ask your tutor to lend it to you.
5. Birthday surprise — Your friend has a new present. Find out what it is and react.
6. The birthday planner — Choose a present for a friend. Agree on one item and a colour.
7. The collection museum — Show your collection to a visitor. A pretend collection is welcome.
8. Swap club — You and your tutor want to swap one collectible. Find a fair choice.
9. Guess my number — Your tutor has a secret collection size. Ask questions to find it.
10. Object detective — Your tutor describes an object without its name. Ask questions and identify it.
11. Near or far? — You direct your tutor to the right object in the room.
12. The souvenir guide — Help a visitor choose a souvenir from the UK.
13. Two gifts, one budget — Buy gifts for two different people. You have £20.
14. Colour, please — Find a souvenir in the colour you want. Ask for another option if necessary.
15. A helpful shop assistant — You are the assistant. Find the right souvenir for your customer.
16. The travel desk — Help a visitor connect a country, a capital and a continent.
17. A postcard home — Imagine a short visit to another country. Tell your tutor what you have got.
18. Pack for a day out — Agree on five things for a day trip. Check what each person has got.
19. Design a souvenir — Create a souvenir for your town. Persuade a visitor to choose it.
20. The one-minute world — Bring the module together in one connected conversation.

Each scenario has a student role, a private tutor role, a choice that changes the conversation, a plot twist, useful language, an optional timer, role switching, tutor feedback and local notes. Open speaking tasks are not automatically graded.

## Listening

The original recordings were not attached. Exercises p.39/9 and p.40/2 retain their original prompts and response choices. ## Accuracy

The textbook geography model names Cape Town. A separate tutor note explains South Africa’s three capitals. The original souvenir hat text says Ireland; the accompanying note distinguishes Northern Ireland from the Republic of Ireland.

## Technical / visual QA

All 48 book activity blocks, all 34 interactive stages, all 20 speaking scenarios and all 11 vocabulary topics in four modes were exercised through DOM-based tests. Correct/incorrect answers, repeat attempts, matching, sorting, Progress Check /100, choices, twists, timer cleanup, persistence and shared Reset were tested. Static desktop/mobile-layout renders of the reading, vocabulary, demonstratives, speaking, studio overview and core overview were inspected. Static renders approximate browser layout; they do not replace real browser/device testing. Browser control and device audio were unavailable in this environment.
